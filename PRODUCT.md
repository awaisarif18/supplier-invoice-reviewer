# Product

<!-- impeccable:product-schema 1 -->

## Platform

web

## Stack

A static, dependency-free browser app with ES modules and Node's test runner. The deployment target is the existing Oracle Ubuntu instance.

## Users

Accounts payable reviewers verifying one invoice line before payment. Hiring managers can reproduce the workflow with bundled samples.

## Product Purpose

Find discrepancies against the contract rate effective on the invoice date, show the supporting records, and let a reviewer record a decision in under a minute.

## Capabilities and Constraints

Three synthetic cases, integer-cent calculations, inspectable source records, and browser-local decisions keyed by invoice line. Check results and human decisions are independent. An unresolved contract rate leaves the expected total and difference blank.
