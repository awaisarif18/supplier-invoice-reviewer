# Product

<!-- impeccable:product-schema 1 -->

## Platform

web

## Stack

React, Vite, TypeScript, and Node’s test runner. The built pages are served as static files on the existing Oracle Ubuntu instance. An optional Node endpoint extracts fields from uploaded PDF text.

## Users

Accounts payable reviewers verifying one invoice line before payment. Hiring managers can reproduce the workflow with bundled samples or use the custom case to try different inputs.

## Product Purpose

Find discrepancies against the contract rate effective on the invoice date, show the supporting records, and let a reviewer record a decision in under a minute.

## Capabilities and Constraints

Three synthetic case pages, an editable case, an upload flow for selectable-text PDFs, integer-cent calculations, inspectable source records, and browser-local decisions keyed by sample invoice line. Check results and human decisions are independent. An unresolved contract rate leaves the expected total and difference unavailable.
